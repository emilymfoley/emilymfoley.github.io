About Me

Welcome to my website! I am a second year Applied Mathematics Ph.D. student at the University of Arizona advised by Kaitlin Kratter, and a DOE Computational Science Fellow. I am broadly interested in the intersection of high performance computing, applied math, and astrophysics. My research focuses on using high fidelity numerical simulations to study star forming regions in the interstellar medium to better understand the feedback mechanisms driving star formation and evolution.

I have also been involed in outreach projects through the Association for Women in Mathematics for three years, both at my alma mater Wake Forest and the University of Arizona.

I am originally from Minneapolis, Minnesota (yes, I have a slight accent, and yes, it is very cold there). I graduated from Wake Forest University in 2024 with B.S. degrees in physics and mathematics, where I worked with Paul Anderson and John Gemmer.
