Current Projects

Graduate Research

I study how protostellar outflows form and evolve in binary and multiple star systems, and how these outflows are determined by magnetic fields during early star formation. The goal of this project is to determine how feedback from young multiple systems regulates star formation in clustered environments. Using magnetohydrodynamic simulations from the STARFORGE project, I identify bound multiple systems and analyze the structure and alignment of their outflows. I am currently focusing on determining whether binaries drive distinct or shared outflows, and how outflow properties differ from those of single stars.
